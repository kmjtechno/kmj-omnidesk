# KMJ TECHNO Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want collaboration in this project to be professional, respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming. Participants should communicate constructively, respect different experience levels and viewpoints, focus criticism on ideas and evidence, and protect other people's privacy and security.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading comments, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, sexualized conduct, or attempts to pressure contributors into unsafe or unethical actions.

## Project spaces

These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication channels, and public interactions where someone is representing this project or KMJ TECHNO.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or otherwise moderate contributions or interactions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Reporting

Do not use a public issue to report sensitive conduct or security matters. Contact KMJ TECHNO through https://kmjtechno.com and provide enough context for the maintainers to review the report. Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion.

## Scope

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Enforcement decisions should be based on observable conduct and project safety, not on disagreement with a technical position.
